SECTION 1: OAMD TEST
================================================================================

OUTCOME
-------
PADDOCK is trialled within the regiment and validated as a fit-for-purpose
digital horse management system that replaces or supplements existing manual
and paper-based record keeping.

ACTION
------
1. Train all designated users (Troopers, NCOs, Vets, Officers) on the app
   prior to the trial start date.
2. Users employ PADDOCK as the primary tool for horse welfare records, injury
   reporting, health scheduling, duty assignments, and location tracking
   throughout the trial period.
3. No reversion to legacy systems/processes during the trial unless a critical
   system failure occurs (in which case the fallback is documented).
4. Feedback is collected continuously and formally at trial midpoint and end.

MEASURE
-------
The trial is successful if ALL of the following are met:

  a) Adoption:     All trained users actively use PADDOCK throughout the trial
                   period with no voluntary reversion to paper/legacy systems.
  b) Accuracy:     Horse records (health events, injuries, locations, duty
                   assignments) in PADDOCK match ground truth with no data
                   loss or critical errors.
  c) Timeliness:   Injury reports are submitted within the same working day.
                   Health schedules (dental, farriery, vet, vaccinations) are
                   maintained up to date with no missed entries.
  d) Usability:    User feedback scores average 3.5/5 or above across all
                   categories (ease of use, reliability, usefulness).
  e) Availability: System uptime of 95% or greater during the trial period
                   (excluding planned maintenance).
  f) Role Coverage: Each role (Trooper, Vet, Officer, Admin) has successfully
                   used their designated features without permission issues.

DEADLINE
--------
  Trial Start:          27 April 2026
  Midpoint Review:      27 May 2026
  Trial End:            29 June 2026
  Feedback Collected:   By 29 June 2026
  Final Report Due:     6 July 2026

SECTION 2: USER TRAINING PLAN
================================================================================

TRAINING AIM
------------
All designated PADDOCK users are confident and competent in using the app for
their role-specific functions before the trial begins on 27 April 2026.

TRAINING AUDIENCE & ROLE MAPPING
---------------------------------
  Role        Typical Rank/Post             Key App Functions
  ----------  ----------------------------  ------------------------------------
  TROOPER     Troopers, LCoH               View horse records, submit injury
                                            reports, attach photos/documents
  VET         Regimental/Visiting Vets      Manage health events, clinical notes,
                                            medications, feeding plans, resolve
                                            injuries, record inspections
  OFFICER     Troop Leaders, Div Comd,      Assign duties, approve horse moves,
              Adjutant                      manage tack/inspections, create
                                            horses, manage locations
  ADMIN       System Administrators         Full access: user management,
                                            locations, system configuration

TRAINING SCHEDULE
-----------------
  Date              Session                     Audience        Duration
  ----------------  --------------------------  --------------  --------
  21 Apr 2026       Session 1: Overview &       All Users       45 min
                    Login/Navigation
  22 Apr 2026       Session 2: Trooper          Troopers        30 min
                    Functions (view, report)
  23 Apr 2026       Session 3: Vet Functions    Vets            45 min
                    (health, meds, inspections)
  24 Apr 2026       Session 4: Officer          Officers        45 min
                    Functions (duties, moves,
                    locations)
  25 Apr 2026       Session 5: Admin Overview   Admins          30 min
                    (users, config)
  25 Apr 2026       Drop-in Troubleshooting     All Users       60 min
  27 Apr 2026       TRIAL BEGINS                --              --

TRAINING DELIVERY METHOD
-------------------------
1. Hands-on demonstration using the live system (or staging environment).
2. Each user logs in on their own device during the session.
3. Walk through core workflows relevant to their role.
4. Provide a one-page quick reference card per role (print & digital).
5. Nominate one "PADDOCK Champion" per troop/section as first point of
   contact for questions during the trial.

TRAINING MATERIALS
------------------
  - Quick Reference Cards (one per role: Trooper, Vet, Officer, Admin)
  - Login credentials issued in advance
  - Short video walkthroughs (screen recordings of key workflows) - optional
  - Contact details for technical support during the trial
